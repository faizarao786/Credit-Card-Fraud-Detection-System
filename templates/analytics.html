Analysis Summary

The analytics dashboard provides a visual overview of the credit card transaction dataset. The pie chart shows the proportion of normal and fraudulent transactions, while the transaction comparison chart compares their total counts. The additional charts show fraud rate, normal rate, transaction amount distribution, and transaction class analysis. These statistics are calculated directly from the current creditcard.csv dataset.
